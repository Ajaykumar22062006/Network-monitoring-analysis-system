Subnetting Terminology Guide

Network Address

Identifies the subnet. All hosts on the same physical link share this prefix. It represents the subnet and is not assignable to devices.

Broadcast Address

A special IP used to send messages to all active hosts in the subnet. Packets directed here are received by every host on the subnet. It cannot be assigned to hosts.

First & Last Usable Host

The boundary range of IPs that can be manually or dynamically (DHCP) assigned to end stations (e.g. PCs, routers, printers).

Usable Hosts Tally

Calculated as 2(32 - prefix) - 2. We subtract 2 because the Network Address and Broadcast Address are reserved and cannot be assigned to hosts.

ARP Learning Guide

What is ARP?

ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) is used on an IPv4 local network to find the MAC address associated with an IP address.

Why is MAC address needed?

IP addresses identify devices logically, while MAC addresses are used for delivery on the local network.

Dynamic vs. Static Entries

Dynamic: Entries created automatically when a device responds to an ARP request. They expire and are cleared if unused for a short period.

Static: Entries added manually or assigned by the network (like multicast/broadcast mappings). They do not expire and remain in the cache until the network device restarts.
